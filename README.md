# Masoud Nazari

### Visual Designer · UI/UX Designer · Digital Product Creator

I'm a multidisciplinary designer with 10+ years of experience in graphic design, brand identity, and visual communication.

Throughout my career, I've worked with businesses and brands to create meaningful visual experiences, from logo design and brand identity systems to product packaging, advertising campaigns, catalogs, and digital interfaces.

My professional journey has gradually expanded into UI/UX design, web development, and AI-assisted application development. I enjoy combining creative thinking, design principles, and modern technologies to transform ideas into practical digital products.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of **Design, Technology, and Artificial Intelligence** — building useful tools, improving user experiences, and exploring new ways to solve real-world problems.
